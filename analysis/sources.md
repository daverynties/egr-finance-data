# Sources — EGR public-records research (accessed 2026-10-07)

Every URL below was accessed on October 7, 2026. Official sources are marked OFFICIAL; secondary sources are marked SECONDARY.

## Millage / tax

1. https://eastgrmi.gov/DocumentCenter/View/37/millagerates?bidId= — OFFICIAL (City of East Grand Rapids). "City of East Grand Rapids Millage Rates" spreadsheet (dated 6/25/2026). Used for: 2026, 2025, 2024 summer millage by authority (EGR school debt/sinking/recreation/operating, Kent ISD, GRCC, State Ed Tax, city operating/roads/complex/parks, transit, county operating) and summer PRE/non-PRE totals. 2026 winter levy not yet published in this document.
2. https://transnationtitle.com/wp-content/uploads/pdfs/millage_rates/2025/KENT-2025.PDF — SECONDARY (title-company compilation of 2025 Kent County millage rates). Used only to cross-check the 2025 annual totals (PRE 47.4995 / non-PRE 65.4995); not relied on for the breakdown.
3. https://www.egrps.org/district-information/elections/2025-enhancement-millage-and-sinking-fund — OFFICIAL (EGR Public Schools). Used for: Nov 4, 2025 sinking-fund ballot language (0.5 mill, 2026-2035, est. $548,320 in 2026) and Kent ISD enhancement millage ballot language (.8498 renewal + .0502 restoration).
4. https://www.schoolnewsnetwork.org/2025/11/05/voters-approve-ballot-measures-in-several-districts-defeat-bond-in-rockford/ — SECONDARY (Kent ISD publication). Used for: unofficial election results reported from Kent County Clerk — EGR sinking fund passed 2,281-844; Kent ISD enhancement millage passed 64,402-37,612. NOT the certified canvass.
5. https://www.kentcountymi.gov/2012/2026-Corrections-and-Detention-Millage — OFFICIAL (Kent County). Used for: Nov 3, 2026 Corrections & Detention millage — 0.98 mill renewal+increase, 2026-2045; 2026 certified rate 0.7332 (vs original 0.84); +0.2468 over certified; $24.68/yr per $100K taxable value; est. $37.33M first-year levy.

## Parking-facility bond proposal

Used for: resolution 2026-147/147-A correcting the bond resolution's average millage (0.44925 → 0.4925); up to $9M bond; Nov 3, 2026 ballot; vote 4-1-2 (Burdick, Schwartz, Skaggs, Favale yes; Wessely no; Hunter, Groff-Blaszak abstain with stated reasons).

Used only as corroboration that the Aug 3, 2026 vote placed the item on the ballot.
