# Context packet: Gaslight Village future plan (East Grand Rapids, MI), Oct 2026

## Real geography (verified against OSM and a Google Maps screenshot)
- Wealthy St SE runs diagonally NW→SE through Gaslight Village (secondary road, the main street).
- The ~8.6-acre Gaslight Investors site (2255 Wealthy St SE / 515 Lakeside Dr SE) is NORTH of Wealthy and WEST of Lakeside Dr.
- Lakeside Dr curves N–S along the west shore of Reeds Lake, just east of the site. Reeds Lake (~280 ac) is immediately east; John Collins Park and the waterfront are at the lake's SW shore near Lakeside/Lake Dr.
- East Grand Rapids High School (and Middle School campus area) is SOUTH of Wealthy, between Wealthy and Lake Dr SE.
- Lake Dr SE runs parallel to Wealthy, further south (a secondary arterial to Grand Rapids).
- Wealthy Elementary is to the WEST along Wealthy. Grid side streets (Greenwood, Bagley, Pinecrest, San Jose, etc.).
- Existing OSM paths: Reeds Lake Trail (footway/cycleway segments around the lake), many sidewalks; little on-street bike infrastructure.

## Site and policy facts
- Site zoned PUD; the 2004 Jade Pig PUD (up to 107 condos, 687 parking spaces, up to 7 stories/94 ft peak) staff says remains valid.
- A 147-unit/493-space redevelopment approved 4–3 on Oct 6 2025 was VOIDED Aug 10 2026 (Judge Quist): resident protest petition valid, needed 2/3 vote (MCL 125.3403).
- Existing Buildings A/B: two stories, ~77,500 sf C-1 retail/office. The old parking deck's upper level was demolished early 2025.
- Nov 3 2026 ballot: up to $9M city borrowing (~0.49 mills) for a public/school parking deck. School memo: senior lot 111→40 spaces; combined surveyed peak occupancy 939/1,310 (72%); school peak 87%.
- Traffic: ~6,900 daily trips estimated for redevelopment; Saturday peaks are higher than 2004 PUD.
- EGR: ~12,000 residents, 3.4 sq mi, affluent, strong schools, engaged and height/density-skeptical residents; Michigan Housing TIF and brownfield tools exist; The Rapid bus serves Wealthy/Lake Dr lightly.
- Stormwater from the site drains to Reeds Lake via a 36-inch system.
- David's earlier "Gaslight Green" concept: 116 homes, 3–4 stories, a 1-acre Village Green deeded to the City, a 300-space wrapped shared garage, ~$14M unfunded gap.
