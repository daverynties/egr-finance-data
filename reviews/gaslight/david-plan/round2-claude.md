## Round 2 critique

**Strongest elements**
- **Grok** has the most practical phasing. Phase 1 builds the garage together with the Wealthy building that wraps it, so the garage isn't left exposed. It also delivers the green first and writes affordability into the PUD agreement as a firm number, not "up to."
- **Codex** has the most honest economics. Land ($4M) and developer return ($5M) are inside the cost, and the TIF is given both as a nominal ceiling and as a present value. It also measures height to the roof peak (50 ft), which closes the "midpoint vs. peak" loophole the 2004 PUD had (79 vs 94 ft).
- **Claude** has the best political mapping. It answers each skeptic's on-record concern (Hunter on density, Skaggs on open space, Groff-Blaszak on process). It builds on the 7–0 Phase 1, pays for green maintenance through a recorded assessment rather than the general fund, and adds an independent "but-for" pro forma review.

**Errors and unrealistic numbers**
- **Grok, missing costs:** cost leaves out developer profit and sales costs, so the "$7M gap" is understated. A realistic figure is about $14–18M.
- **Grok, unit size:** 125k gross sq ft for 120 units is about 1,040 sq ft each. That can't produce $650k for-sale homes, which need about 1,250–2,150 sq ft at $303–530/sq ft (CE §4).
- **Grok, deck money:** redirecting deck-bond money ($2–4M) to a private easement goes beyond what the Nov 3 ballot authorizes.
- **Grok, affordability:** "15% (11) + 5 units" is ambiguous. It could mean 11 or 16 units.
- **Codex, sale prices:** the math checks out ($70.4M value, $6.6M gap). But a $800k average across 72 sale homes works out to about $515/sq ft net. That is the top of the comparable range, needs heavy absorption, and has only a thin 6.5% return.
- **Codex, layout:** 180 surface stalls take about 1.4 acres. That collides with the 1.25-acre green and the Master Plan's "screened parking."
- **Codex, retail:** 8k sq ft is too small for a pharmacy.
- **Claude, townhome garages:** it lists 60 garages for 18 townhomes, which is 3.3 each. It should be about 36.
- **Claude, TIF size:** a **$12M PV** TIF over 15 years needs about $1.24M a year at 6%, which is close to the parcel's entire projected increment while phasing ramps up. It is also about $1M per affordable unit, against roughly $140k in Lowell (CP §5).
- **Claude, deed restriction:** 15 years is weak.
- **Claude, phasing:** Phase 1 puts the garage ahead of its Phase 2 wrap building, which leaves the garage exposed and carried by only 40 units of revenue.

**Joint plan: the 7 decisions**
1. **Units:** 118 homes (13.7/acre). That is 50 elevator condos, 18 townhomes and 50 rentals (Claude).
2. **Height:** 3 stories / 40 ft at the edges and on Lakeside. 4 stories / 50 ft *including peaks* only at the core, with a 12-ft step-back (Codex).
3. **Green:** 1.0-acre Village Green deeded to the city (easement as fallback), plus a 0.3-acre plaza. Maintenance is paid by a recorded assessment, and a letter of credit guarantees it before Phase 2.
4. **Parking:** about 395 spaces: a 300-stall wrapped garage, 60 surface stalls and 36 townhome garages.
   - Each home gets 1.25 reserved spaces (about 148). The rest are shared, including the school-event clause.
   - None of it depends on the deck bond.
   - A school-peak study is required before the vote.
5. **Retail:** 15,000 sq ft new (Claude), for a restaurant, café and pharmacy.
6. **Affordability:** 16 rentals (32%): 8 at ≤80% AMI and 8 at ≤100% AMI, for 30 years, through Kent BRA Housing TIF.
7. **Phasing and incentives:**
   - Phase 1: the garage, its Wealthy wrap building and the green (Grok).
   - Phase 2: Lakeside condos and townhomes.
   - TIF capped at **$8M nominal (about $4.6–5M PV)**, published as a single number.
   - The remaining gap of about $8–10M, using Codex's method with land and return included, is closed by an owner land concession plus grants for the green. No general-fund cash.

**What would cost the 5th vote**
- Any TIF number near Claude's $12M, given the circulating (unsourced) "$20M+" TIF claim (FB).
- Anything that looks like double-dipping on the deck bond (Grok).
- A privately titled green or visible surface lots (Codex).
- A Phase 1 that builds the garage before the homes and green that justify it.
- Releasing the pro forma, renderings and traffic study fewer than 30 days before the vote.
