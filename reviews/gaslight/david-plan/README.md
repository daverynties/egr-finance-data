# David's alternative plan for the Gaslight site: "Gaslight Green"
Concept proposal for the 8.6-acre Gaslight Investors / former Jade Pig site (2255 Wealthy St SE / 515 Lakeside Dr SE). The land is privately owned, so this is a vision and a negotiating basis unless the owner engages. Prepared Oct 9, 2026.

**Headline:** 116 homes; 3 stories at the edges and 4 stories / 50 ft at the core; a 1.0-acre public Village Green; 400 parking spaces (300 in a wrapped garage); 12k sq ft of new retail; 15 rentals restricted for 30 years. Screening gap: about $27.4M. After TIF, a negotiated owner concession and a green grant, about **$14M remains unfunded** (see PLAN.md, "Unfunded gap and what would have to be true").
