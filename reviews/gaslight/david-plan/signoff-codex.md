**Position: ENDORSE WITH RESERVATIONS**

I endorse the village-green concept as a basis for negotiation, conditional on owner participation, verified site fit and committed financing.

The principal counts check: **116 homes, 13.5/acre, 21.1% fewer than 147; 56.9% ownership; 400 spaces; 145 reserved and 255 shared.**

Together with $2.52M garage savings and $1.77M residential savings, the levers yield **$6.95M**, leaving about **$0.35M** of the stated $7.3M gap before overflow-parking costs. The 32 townhome stalls must count within residential reservations; school-event availability needs simultaneous-peak verification.

Reeds Lake lies east of the site, so correct the southward lake-axis description. The [2004 PUD already included school-event parking obligations](/workspace/gaslight/sources/Revised-PUD-amendment-memo.txt:1434); “never honored” requires evidence.
