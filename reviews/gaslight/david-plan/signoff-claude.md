I've checked the plan's arithmetic against the research files. Here is the sign-off.

**Position: ENDORSE WITH RESERVATIONS**

**Numbers that check out:** 116 homes (13.5/acre, 57% for sale, 21% fewer than 147). Height is 20 ft below the voided plan's 70 ft and 44 ft below the 94 ft peak. The 15 affordable units are 30% of rentals. 400 parking spaces, with 145 reserved (1.25 per home). The cost total ($85.1M), proceeds ($64.6M), gap ($20.5M), fills ($13.2M) and remaining gap ($7.3M) all reconcile within rounding. Market rentals only come to $11.7M with an unstated 5% vacancy; without it the figure is $12.3M. That assumption should be stated.

**Reservations**
1. **Garage cost may be low.** The plan assumes $42k per stall. The City's own Nov 3 bond estimates about **$60k per space** for the Bagley deck (CP, PD). At $60k, 300 stalls cost about **$5.4M more** in hard costs, roughly $7M with soft costs and return. That would push the remaining gap toward ~$14M. Use the local figure, or explain why this garage would be cheaper.
2. **The three levers barely close the gap.** As written they add up to $7.1M, which is less than $7.3M. Once soft costs and return are counted on the cost cuts, they get there, but only if all three succeed. Also, the condo lever is +$2.66M after the 5% sales cost, not +$2.8M. The plan should say plainly that it needs every lever to work.
3. **The 240-stall lever conflicts with the parking commitment.** It would cut the 255 shared spaces by about a quarter. Parking would then be 93–150 below the voided plan's 493, against a 2004 entitlement of 687, and residents already doubt that parking is adequate.
4. **The owner concession may be unrealistic.** Free land plus a 3.5% return leaves the owner worse off than building under its 2004 rights. That makes "owner declines" the most likely risk, not just a listed one.
5. **TIF per affordable unit is high.** $5M PV across 15 units is about **$333k per unit**, versus about $140k at Lowell. Expect this to come up at the County BRA.

**Factual corrections:** None material. The 70 ft and 94 ft heights, 4–3 vote, 7–0 Phase 1, Nov 3 bond (up to $9M) and the commissioner quotes match the README and the community-precedents file.
