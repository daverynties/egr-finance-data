# Round 1 — Grok (city-leadership decision memo)

**Decision.** Proceed *with binding conditions and a size check*: let the Nov 3 vote stand, but commit publicly, by resolution, that no bonds will be sold until a signed city–school agreement, an itemized estimate and a funded operating plan exist, and price a two-level/plain-façade option before choosing what to build.

**Why**
- The campus problem is real but narrow: the expansion removes 71 spaces (Nov 10, 2025 memo) and drops campus to ~65 spaces in 2028–30 (Sept 30 letter, via dossier). Area-wide, 371 of 1,310 spaces were empty at the 2025 peak (utilization CSV).
- Our own engineer ranked a structure last (memo, Nov 10, 2025); the Feb 4 follow-up names no infeasible option. Schwartz: "the school needs two levels and the third is a possibility for the City" (Jul 20 minutes). The city's money mainly buys level 3.
- Nothing is binding: no signed agreement (FOX 17, Aug 4), free public use only promised to school-event attendees (Sept 30 letter), no operating line in the budget, and $9M + ~$7M ≈ $16M sits below most OAK estimates ($15.25M–$18.11M) — an unexplained funding gap (inferred).

**Conditions (gates before any bond sale)**
1. Signed intergovernmental agreement: capital split, cap on city share, overrun responsibility, O&M and reserve split, public hours, no-fee public use, 3-ft right-of-way.
2. OAK itemized estimate and bids published; borrow only what bids require.
3. Priced two-level / Option 5 alternative and middle-school-lot cost, compared side by side.
4. Operating + capital-reserve line in the budget (range $56K–$147K/yr before reserve; inferred).
5. Pedestrian-safety project list with costs.

**Next steps**
- *Before Nov 3* (City Manager + City Attorney, next regular meeting — date to confirm): adopt a resolution stating the gates; publish the itemized estimate, $7M status and O&M estimate as factual information only (campaign-finance limits on advocacy: to confirm with city attorney).
- *If it passes* (City Manager, by Feb 2027): negotiate IGA; rebid with Option 5 and two-level alternates; commission votes on size and issue amount only after bids; publish Phase 2 (2028–30) interim parking plan with EGRPS.
- *If it fails* (City Manager + EGRPS, by Jan 2027): treat as "not yet"; work the engineer's sequence (on-street management, middle-school lot, bussing); return no earlier than with a signed IGA and itemized costs.

**Risks**
- Delay raises cost (~3.5%/yr escalation; inferred from OAK 2026→2028) — mitigate by pre-negotiating the IGA now.
- Deck misses the 2028–30 crunch (design paused at 50%) — require a schedule in the IGA.
- 30–60 min load-out pushes students back to streets — exit design as a gate.
- Schools can build alone (FOX 17) — keep talks open either way.

**What would change it.** A credible schedule showing the deck opens before 2030 plus a signed IGA → proceed as planned. Bids far above $18M or no IGA by bid time → don't issue; downsize or stop.
