Price two versus three levels separately. ([Packet findings §7](/workspace/parking-council/inputs/research/parking-deck-aug3-packet-findings.md:100))
- The funding gap is conditional. The $15.247M plain option in 2026 dollars fits within $16M combined; the $18.110M brick option in 2028 dollars exceeds it by $2.110M **(inferred)**. Neither comparison establishes secured school funding or compliance with an equal-share agreement. Use consistent construction-year dollars and confirmed contributions. ([Packet findings §3](/workspace/parking-council/inputs/research/parking-deck-aug3-packet-findings.md:36))
- Neither operating range is a validated project budget. Grok’s $56K includes a sinking fund in 2018 dollars; $147K extrapolates a different O&M benchmark. Claude’s $300K–$330K adds an assumed 1% reserve **(inferred)**; possible overlap needs checking. ([Fact-check, items 060–072](/workspace/parking-council/inputs/tonight/codex-factcheck-2026-10-08.md:114))
- “Binding conditions by resolution” remains a proposed safeguard. Whether it constrains future commissions, bond issuance or redesign is **(to confirm with city attorney)**; the supplied review does not verify original ballot wording. ([Fact-check, items 001–004](/workspace/parking-council/inputs/tonight/codex-factcheck-2026-10-08.md:50))
