# Round 2 — Claude

**Where I agree**
- All three of us land in the same place: no bond sale until there's a signed city–school agreement (IGA), an itemized estimate, and bids. Borrow only what the bids require.
- Grok is right that the city's money mostly buys the third level ("the school needs two levels," Jul 20 minutes). Pricing a two-level design and Option 5 side by side should be a formal gate.
- Codex is right that the 371 spare spaces system-wide don't help much near the school. The school lots reached 86.8% on a Saturday (utilization CSV), so I'm giving less weight to my "need is narrow" argument.
- The 2028–30 shortfall (~65 spaces) needs an interim plan whether or not the measure passes.

**Where I disagree and why**
- **Codex's "pause / alternatives first" label.** Codex keeps the measure on the ballot and allows building if the conditions are met. That's in practice the same as proceeding with conditions. Calling it a pause while the question is live could confuse voters. Whether the city can pull or change a certified question is to confirm with city attorney.
- **Grok's funding gap.** $9M + ~$7M ≈ $16M is above the low OAK figure ($15.25M). The real risk is on the school's side: its half could fall up to ~$2.1M short of the top estimate (inferred). The IGA should make the school responsible for its own overruns.
- **My own O&M range.** My $300K–$330K figure assumed a reserve level the inputs don't support. I now use Codex's figure: $146,640/yr before reserve (inferred from the $611/space benchmark). Treat any reserve amount as an open item until an engineer estimates it.
- **Codex's Oct 26 resolution date.** It's only 8 days before the election. Aim for the next regular meeting, and no later than Oct 19 if that date works (date to confirm).

**Revised position**
Proceed with conditions, and add a downsize check: commit to a two-level, Option 5 design unless the school pays for level 3 and brick, or the IGA guarantees enforceable free public hours. The other gates stay: IGA, itemized bids, and a funded O&M line. If the measure fails, run the engineer's sequence (on-street parking first, then the middle-school lot) plus the busing pilot by Jan 2027.
