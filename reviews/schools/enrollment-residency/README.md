# EGRPS enrollment by residency

Raw exports from MI School Data, "Schools of Choice and Other Non-Resident Enrollments" and "Student Count" trend reports, East Grand Rapids Public Schools (district 41090):
https://www.mischooldata.org/schools-of-choice-and-other-non-resident-enrollments/

- `egr_nonresident_*.csv`: nonresident students by sending district. Use the `Summary` rows (column "Number of Nonresident Students Enrolled") for district totals; summing every row double-counts.
- `egr_student_count_*.csv`: total K–12 headcount by year.
- `findings.md`: earlier research notes, including Census under-18 population sources.

Figures used on site/index.html (#school-residency):

| Year | Total | Nonresident | % |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2009–10 | 2,985 | 295 | 9.9% |
| 2015–16 | 2,938 | 259 | 8.8% |
| 2019–20 | 2,887 | 263 | 9.1% |
| 2021–22 | 2,889 | 341 | 11.8% |
| 2023–24 | 2,929 | 395 | 13.5% |
| 2025–26 | 2,975 | 433 | 14.6% |

Caveat: the state breaks out Schools of Choice separately only for 2015–16 to 2020–21; later totals combine Schools of Choice, tuition and other nonresident routes. Headcount, not class size.
